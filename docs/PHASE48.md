# Phase 48 — Billing ledger foundation

Owner-only creation and listing of **draft internal invoice records** tied to existing subscription and school through server-side foreign-key resolution. Amounts use INR paise. All new invoices remain `draft` and have no issued/paid date. No gateway callback, payment capture, payment statuses, taxes, GST registration, refund, reconciliation or formal legal invoice delivery exists. This is **not complete payments/invoices/renewals** and must not be presented as payable or tax-compliant. Subsequent production billing requires provider-authenticated webhooks, idempotency, audited double-entry or reconciled ledger, tax rules, invoice numbering and proper customer notifications. Run `php artisan test --filter=Phase48SaasInvoicesTest` and full CI.
